In a concentrated book, false confidence is the expensive failure. ## TF4 — Research Production & the Agentic Layer

**(a) First principles.** Research production converts raw engine facts into a defensible BUY/HOLD/ROTATE/KILL judgment on a concentrated, asymmetric book — and does it so the operator can trust WHY, not just WHAT. The purpose splits cleanly: the engine owns everything reproducible and unargued (price, REP floor, T/Q/V, ρ/φ, JSF, MRI, posture, sizing) so two runs give the same number and nobody negotiates the math; the agentic layer owns everything genuinely contested (discovery, synthesis, verification/disconfirmation, the dialectic); the human owns the trigger on state mutation.
